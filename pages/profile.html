Aerobic sessions

DateWorkoutSteadyDecouplingVT1 (α1)
Steady Z1–Z2 runs and rides with 20 min or more past the warm-up and its first 5 min. Decoupling: how much efficiency falls from the first half to the second, flat-equivalent speed ÷ HR on a run (Pa:HR), watts ÷ HR on a ride (Pw:HR); under 5 % the aerobic base held for that effort, over 10 % it was too much that day. VT1 is estimated from α1 (chest strap), is lower on the bike than running and varies from day to day: watch each trend.
